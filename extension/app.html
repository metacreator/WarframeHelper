    Welcome, Tenno!

    Three steps to turn what you don't need into platinum:

    1. 1
      Log in on warframe.market In this browser, with Steam, Google, Discord or a password. This page notices it by itself.
    2. 2
      Scan your inventory Take screenshots of your in-game inventory and drop them below, or add items by hand.
    3. 3
      See prices and list See what each item is worth and put it on warframe.market in one click.

    Scan screenshots

    Drop screenshots here, click to choose files, or paste with Ctrl+V

    Open your in-game Inventory (or the Mods screen), take screenshots while scrolling, and drop them here. Overlapping screenshots are fine: an item seen in several screenshots is only counted once. If your game isn't in English, pick its language below first. Tip: Win+Shift+S then Ctrl+V on this page.
