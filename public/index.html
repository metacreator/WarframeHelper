Import a game inventory export (JSON)

Accepts the raw Warframe inventory JSON (with MiscItems, Recipes, RawUpgrades, Upgrades) saved by an inventory export tool. Items are matched to the market by their internal game path. Built gear is skipped since it can't be traded.

Scan screenshots

Open your in-game Inventory (or the Mods screen), take screenshots while scrolling, and drop them here. Overlapping screenshots are fine: an item seen in several screenshots is only counted once. Tip: Win+Shift+S then Ctrl+V on this page.
